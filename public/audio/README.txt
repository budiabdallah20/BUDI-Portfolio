AZAN AUDIO
=========

Drop your azan clip here as:

  public/audio/azan.mp3

~5 seconds is enough — the dashboard plays the first 5 seconds with a
gentle fade (e.g. "Allahu Akbar" in the voice of Sheikh Abdul Basit
Abdul Samad).

If the file is missing, a soft chime plays instead — nothing breaks.
This folder is git-ignored for mp3s except .gitkeep (keep your audio
private if you prefer).
